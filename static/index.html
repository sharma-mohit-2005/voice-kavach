      Frequently Asked Questions
      
      Forensic Verification Standards
      
      
      Clear answers regarding accuracy, privacy, file requirements, and legal citations.
      
    
      

    
      
      
      
        How accurate is the detector?
        
      
          On clean, uncompressed audio clips, the detector reports 99% accuracy across 24+ generator models.
          On heavily compressed phone line audio (such as 8 kHz G.711 telephony), confidence is reported lower to reflect channel limitations.
          We always report the exact confidence level and never claim certainty we do not have.
        
      
      
      
      
      
        What happens to the uploaded audio?
        
      
          Nothing is ever written to disk. The audio file is decoded in memory, evaluated against the acoustic cue model,
          and dropped immediately from RAM. No user voice data is stored, cached, or used to train any model.
        
      
      
      
      
      
        How long does an audio clip need to be?
        
      
          As little as 1 second of speech can be analyzed, with 4 to 15 seconds giving optimal forensic confidence.
          Clips up to 60 seconds are supported in the demo interface.
        
      
      
      
      
      
        Can this be cited in regulatory or compliance audits?
        
      
          Yes. Every result includes an explainable breakdown of acoustic cues, physical measurements (jitter, shimmer, HNR dB,
          high-frequency energy percentage), and a complete JSON machine-readable audit trail stamped with model and calibration versions.
        
      
      
      
      
      
        Can it detect cloned voices over telephone or WhatsApp recordings?
        
      
          Yes. The detector includes narrowband telephony gating that detects whether audio was upsampled from 8 kHz,
          adjusts bandwidth expectations accordingly, and flags anomalies typical of real-time vishing injection attacks.
